What is NairaTrader?

NairaTrader is a Nigerian prop firm that funds traders with virtual capital in exchange for a share of the profits. Traders pay a one-time challenge fee, prove their skill across two evaluation phases, and receive a funded account to trade with real payouts.

The firm is positioned squarely for the Nigerian market, pricing its challenge fees in Naira and offering account sizes starting at ₦200,000 all the way up to ₦1,000,000 and beyond.

⚠️ The trailing drawdown — most important rule

The 20% drawdown is not fixed from your starting balance. As your equity grows, the floor rises with it. If you grow from ₦200,000 to ₦260,000, your floor moves to ₦220,000. A normal losing streak that was safe at the start can breach the account later. Use the calculator to model this precisely.
